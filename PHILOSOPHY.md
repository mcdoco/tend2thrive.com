# Tend2Thrive

## Why Tend2Thrive

> *Who will you do your best work with?*

> *If you don't talk to someone, why are they even in your network?*

Most networking tools are built around accumulation. More connections, more followers, more reach. Tend2Thrive is built around the opposite idea: a smaller, tended network is more valuable than a large, neglected one.

Knowing ***when*** you last spoke with someone is useful. Knowing ***where you met them*** and ***when you last saw them*** is richer still. Tend2Thrive is the memory your network deserves.

## What we are

A **Professional Relationship Manager (PRM)** - a CRM for people, not deals.

---

## What we believe

### Intentionality over volume

Every contact you add is a deliberate choice. We support importing from LinkedIn, but you can't connect in bulk - only in person. The friction of adding someone manually is a feature. It asks you to decide whether they belong.

### Visibility, not judgment

Connections don't get deleted by the system - they go dormant. We show you when you last spoke with someone and let you decide what that means.

### Access is earned, not assumed

The only way to get full access to Tend2Thrive is to be invited in person by someone already on the platform. Invites are scarce by design - earned through activity, not handed out freely. This isn't gatekeeping for its own sake. It encodes the core philosophy at the point of entry: the people here are here because someone vouched for them face to face.

An invite is generated in the moment of meeting and expires quickly. It is only consumed when the invitee completes signup. A failed or ignored attempt costs the inviter nothing.

### Discoverable on your terms

Visibility into your network is opt-in, not assumed. You control whether others can find you and what they can see. Being discoverable is a choice you make deliberately, not a default we set on your behalf.

### We don't own the medium

We are an aggregator and curator, not a communication platform. We intentionally avoid owning the channel through which networking happens. Our value is in what we surface and when - not in capturing your conversations.

### You own your history

You can add, edit, or delete anything - including your full interaction history. If something is deleted, we may note that a deletion occurred, but the content is gone permanently.

Your data is yours to take. We support export via CSV, API, and agentic integrations - use it however you like, with whatever tools you prefer.

If you want to leave entirely, you can **retract**. Retracting deletes everything: your profile, your history, your connections. You were never here.

## User Flows

### Signing up

Signing up is as easy as logging in with LinkedIn. However, you don't get full access to the product until you've been invited in person by someone already on the platform.

In the meantime, you are able to add contacts manually by entering their LinkedIn profile URL, or uploading your `Connections.csv` from the LinkedIn data export. If someone you've added is already on Tend2Thrive and has also added you, we'll notify them that you're waiting. 

### The in-person handoff

A Tend2Thrive user scans your LinkedIn QR code inside the app. This adds you as a contact and marks the moment you met. In the moment, the scanner sees a **Sign up?** CTA — tapping it shares a link that puts you in the waitlist. No invite is spent; no pressure on either side.

The decision to invite happens later, surfaced in the inviter's app. A connection between a platform user and a waitlisted user can be in one of three states:

- **Wants invite** — both sides have added each other. The platform user is notified that this person is waiting.
- **Met, able to invite** — the platform user has scanned this person in person and has invites available. The app surfaces the option to invite at any time.
- **Invited** — an invite has been sent. A single-use link is generated, expires within minutes, and is only consumed when the scannee completes signup. A failed or ignored attempt costs the inviter nothing.

Once the scannee signs up, the connection becomes **Connected** — a full account, with the full history of how they got there preserved in the timeline.

### Timeline

The Timeline shows your history in reverse chronological order. It lets you contextualize connections - for example, noting the event where you met. For each interaction you can:

- **Thumbs up / thumbs down** - visible only to you, used down the line for matchmaking.
- **Flag a user** - visible to you, the flagged user, and anyone mutually connected to both of you
- **Add private notes** - visible only to you

You are also able to see a timeline shared between you and any of your connections. Here, you can also manage notes and flags.

### Scanner

The primary way to connect and invite people. A simple two-tab view: **Scan** and **Be Scanned**. By default, Be Scanned shows your LinkedIn QR code - if the person scanning you is a Tend2Thrive user, the connection will still be recorded on their end. If not, the time you showed your code is recorded so you can correlate it with when they connected to you on LinkedIn.

## What comes next

The CRM is the foundation. Once it's working well, the natural next step is **matchmaking** - helping people find new connections worth adding to their network in the first place.

The matchmaking loop feeds the CRM: a new connection gets made, then tended. The philosophy stays the same - quality, intentionality, and user control - just applied earlier in the relationship lifecycle.
