Why hosts

Run events people remember

The best events end with people who were genuinely glad to meet - then walk out and forget. Tend2Thrive gives your guests a lasting record of who they met, hands you the introductions that matter, and shows you the connections your event created.

🤝

Broker the introductions

See who's opted in to meet, then introduce two people in a tap - each gets your note on why, in the app. Warm intros during your event, not left to chance and a name tag.

🌱

Your event, the common thread

Guests join through your event and make their connections during it - so it's the room every one of those relationships traces back to.

📈

See the connections you created

Networking is usually invisible. Tend2Thrive surfaces how many connections were made at your event - a real number that shows the room worked.

📶

A shared memory of the room

Attendees leave with a real record of everyone they met - who, where, and how to reach them - instead of a pile of cards they'll never sort.

🔒

Their data, their rules

Attendees control what they share and with whom. You provide the room and the introductions; they own the relationships that come out of it.

✉️

No app store, no friction

Guests sign in with an email magic link or a 6-digit code and install it from the browser. Nothing to download in the doorway.

How it works

Set it up once, then just host

We handle setup. From there it's invite, see who's up to meet, and make the intros.

1

Set up your event

We get your organization and your event set up — cover image, details, and all.

2

Send magic links

Invite guests with magic links. One tap creates their Tend2Thrive profile and adds your event to it — no password, no signup form, no app store.

3

See who's up to meet

Attendees mark themselves available for matchmaking, so you get a live view of exactly who in the room wants an introduction.

4

Make the introductions

Pair up two people who should meet, and each gets your intro — with your note on why — right there in the app.
