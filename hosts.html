How it works

Set it up once, then just host

Reach out and we'll set you up. From there you run everything - events, attendees, and a live dashboard - through our UI or API.

1

Reach out to get set up

Get in touch and we'll create your organization. That's the only step we do for you - everything after is yours to run.

2

Add your events

Create events - in a venue or fully virtual - and fill in the details through our UI or our API, whichever fits how you work.

3

Bring your attendees

Import an attendee CSV, add people by hand, or just share one join link - each gets a ticket, no signup form. Your invites unfurl properly wherever you paste them, and anyone who shows up unannounced checks in as a walk-in at the door.

4

Track the room live

A live board moves each guest through the stages - invited, opened, ready, checked in, left - with a lane for walk-ins. Drag a card to check someone in or mark them gone, and print a thermal name badge for anyone who needs one at the door. It's also where you send the introductions you lined up.

5

See what the room did

Afterward, get the stats that matter - how many people made connections, and whether your introductions were well met or not of interest.
