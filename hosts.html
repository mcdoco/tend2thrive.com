Why hosts

Run events people remember

Most events end with people who were excited to meet - but then they walk out and forget all the details. Tend2Thrive can help.

🕒

See the room before the doors open

Two hours before your event, guests can see who else is coming. They arrive with people they already want to meet - so the room is warm before the first handshake, not after the last one.

🎟️

A ticket, not a signup

Each guest gets a lightweight, ephemeral profile - just an avatar, name, and headline. Enough to see the room and be seen in it, with none of the "create an account" friction that keeps people out. It grows into a full profile only if they want it to.

📲

Check in on any platform, no lock-in

Guests check themselves in by scanning a code - you don't scan their pass. That takes check-in off any single ticketing platform's rails, so your event can live on Luma, Partiful, or wherever you already run it.

🤝

Broker the introductions

See who's opted in to meet, then introduce two people in a tap - each gets your note on why, in the app. Go ahead and play matchmaker.

🌱

Your event, the common thread

Guests join through your event and make their connections during it - so it's the room every one of those relationships traces back to.

📈

See the connections you created

Networking is usually invisible. Tend2Thrive surfaces how many connections were made at your event - a real number that shows the room worked.

How it works

Set it up once, then just host

Reach out and we'll set you up. From there you run everything — events, attendees, and a live dashboard — through our UI or API.

1

Reach out to get set up

Get in touch and we'll create your organization. That's the only step we do for you — everything after is yours to run.

2

Add your events

Create events and fill in the details through our UI or our API — whichever fits how you work.

3

Bring your attendees

Import an attendee CSV or add people by hand. Each gets a ticket — an ephemeral profile, no signup form.

4

Track the room live

A dashboard shows where every attendee stands — invited, accepted the ticket, checked into the event, or left.

5

See what the room did

Afterward, get the stats that matter — how many people made connections, and whether your introductions were well met or not of interest.
