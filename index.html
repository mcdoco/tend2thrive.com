Our philosophy

What we believe

A smaller, tended network beats a large, neglected one. Every decision we've made follows from that.

Intentionality over volume

Every contact you add is a deliberate choice. The friction is a feature — it asks you to decide whether they belong.

Visibility, not judgment

Connections go dormant, not deleted. We show you when you last spoke and let you decide what that means.

Access is earned, not assumed

Invites are scarce by design. Everyone on the platform was vouched for in person. That trust starts at the door.

Discoverable on your terms

Visibility is opt-in, never assumed. You control whether others can find you and what they can see.

We don't own the medium

We surface who to reach out to. How you connect — and what you say — is entirely up to you.

You own your history

Export via CSV, API, or agentic integrations. Or retract entirely — your profile, history, and connections deleted permanently.
