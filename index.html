How it works

From first hello to real relationship, step by step

Here's what happens once you're in.

1

Start with just your name

When your invite lands, sign in with the email you left us - no password, no app store, no signup form. Give your name and you're in.

2

Meet the room you arrived in

Your invite starts you at a networking event. Browse who else is there and privately mark anyone you'd like to meet - if you mark each other, a chat opens. A one-sided mark stays invisible, so there's nothing to lose by trying.

3

Add everyone else you meet

From then on it works wherever you are: scan a code in person, or add timeline@tend2thrive.com as a guest on any calendar invite - the event lands on your timeline, and everyone else on it becomes a contact.

4

Remember what matters

Keep private notes on each person, save the details they share, and see every encounter laid out in order.

5

Follow up with context

When you're ready to reach out, you've got the details - where you met, what you talked about, and a running thread with each person to pick the conversation back up. Message them right here, or take the details and reach out anywhere. A handful of tended relationships beats a thousand forgotten follows.
